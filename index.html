What can happen with too little sleep?

Regularly getting less sleep than you need can affect different parts of your day.

🧠

Harder to focus

Paying attention and staying focused in class can become more difficult.

🔋

Less energy

You may feel tired and have less motivation during the day.

😕

Mood changes

You may become more irritable or feel more stressed.

🛡️

Immune support

Not enough sleep can affect normal immune system function.

📚

Memory

Sleep helps your brain process and remember what you learned.

❤️

Long-term health

Ongoing insufficient sleep is linked with several health risks.

🌙 The 8-Hour Edge

Teenagers generally need about 8–10 hours of sleep each night. Good sleep supports learning, mood, energy and wellbeing.

The 8-Hour Edge: Sleep More, Score More
Better sleep supports a healthier, happier school day.
